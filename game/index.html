Streets of the Sun — an open-world crime story

Danny "Dice" Marlow came to Solmar Bay with a stolen car and a pocket full of nothing. Now fixer Vera Santos has work for him — if he can stay ahead of the Solmar Bay Police.

On foot: WASD / arrows to move  •  Shift to sprint  •  Mouse to aim  •  Click to shoot  •  E to enter a car, pick things up, talk

Driving: W accelerate  •  S brake / reverse  •  A/D steer  •  Space handbrake  •  E to get out
